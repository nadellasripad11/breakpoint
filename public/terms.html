<- breakpoint

terms

last updated 20 november 2026.

what this is

breakpoint is a hackathon project that helps you see how a schedule holds up when something goes wrong. it is provided as is, free of charge, with no promise that it is always available or error-free.

plan with your own judgement

breakpoint's calculations are exact for the information you give it, but they are only as good as that information. do not rely on it for anything where a mistake would cause serious harm, and check important commitments yourself.

your calendars and your links

you may only import calendars and notion pages that you are allowed to share. a calendar link is a secret: anyone who has it can read that calendar, so keep it private and reset it if it leaks. breakpoint only reads, and never changes your calendars or notion.

fair use

please do not try to overload the service, probe other people's data, or use it to attack anything else. access may be limited or removed if that happens.

your data

how data is handled is described in the privacy page. guest data is deleted automatically after 24 hours.

changes and contact

these terms may change as the project grows. questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/nadellasripad11/breakpoint/issues.
